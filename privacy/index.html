Purl Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Purl is a knitting and crochet companion app. This policy explains what Purl does and does not do with your information. In short: Purl keeps your data locally on your device or in your local cloud storage. There are no accounts, there is no server that stores your data, and nothing is sold or shared.

What is collected

Nothing is sent or in any way transferred to us. Everything you put into Purl (your projects, yarn stash, patterns, photos, counters, notes, charts and settings) is stored on your device, in the app's local storage. If you have turned on iCloud sync or Google Drive sync, it is also stored in your own iCloud or Google Drive. The data never leaves your phone or tablet unless you choose to export or share it with others, or you have turned on sync in the app.

With Google Drive sync, Purl asks only for access to its own data in your Google Drive, not to your other files. Purl uses that access only to store and fetch your library so it stays the same on your devices, and shares it with no one. Neither we nor other apps can reach that data, and Purl does not see your name or your email address.

There are no user accounts, and no analytics or tracking. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or usage data.

Camera

Purl can use your device's camera to scan a yarn ball-band barcode so it can fill in the yarn's details for you. The image is processed on your device to read the barcode; no photo or camera image is uploaded or stored by us.

The camera can also take photos for your projects or patterns, or photos to make a chart from. The photos you take are stored only on your device, and in your own iCloud or Google Drive if you have turned on sync.

You can use the whole app without giving Purl access to the camera. Taking photos for your projects, making charts from photos and scanning ball bands are all optional.

Photos

When you add a photo to a project or a pattern, Purl copies it into the app's own storage on your device so it stays with that item. Photos are not uploaded to our servers. If you have turned on sync, your photos are uploaded to your own iCloud or Google Drive. They travel anywhere else only if you create a backup or share file.

Backups and sharing

Purl lets you export your data to a file (a .purl backup, or a .purlp / .purlt share) to move it to another device. These files are created only when you ask, and go wherever you send them (to for example friends and family). We never receive a copy.

Purchases and tips

Purl is free. If you choose to leave an optional tip through the "Support Purl" screen, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or App Store. Purl never receives your card details or any other payment information, so we have no access to your payment details. Google Play or the App Store handles everything and only tells the app whether the purchase completed. A tip unlocks nothing; every feature stays free for everyone.

Feedback and other contact with Purl

If you want to send us feedback from the app, it opens a short external form (Google Forms). The form is anonymous: it does not ask for your name, email address or anything else that identifies you. To help us fix things, the app fills in what you wrote in your note in the app and which app version you have. Neither identifies you, and you choose whether to send the form. Please do not include personal data in the form.

If you do write personal data in the form, Purl is the data controller for your personal data. Our legal basis for processing the information in your message is then our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation). Our legitimate interest, and our purpose, is to get feedback on the app so that we can build a better service for you.

You can also contact us by email. If you email us, we process your email address and any other personal data you give us in our conversation. Our legal basis for processing the information in our conversation with you is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation). Our interest and purpose is to answer your message and help you with what you ask. As with the form, it is also to get feedback on the app so that we can build a better service for you.

We regularly assess whether any personal data from the form or from email correspondence needs to be kept. Personal data that we do not need to handle your message is deleted as soon as we find it.

Other personal data needed for the contact (for example your email address) is kept for as long as the contact with you goes on. When the contact ends, the data is kept until the purpose of your message has been met. If the purpose was feedback to improve the app, the data is deleted once the suggestion has been assessed and, if chosen, built. In any case, the data is as a rule deleted six months after the contact ends.

App updates

Purl checks for updates so you get fixes and improvements. When the app starts, it looks for updates to its own code on our update server. Full app updates are delivered by Google Play or the App Store.

Children

Purl does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Access: You have the right to know whether Purl processes your personal data. You also have the right to a copy of any personal data Purl processes about you.

Rectification: You can ask us to correct or complete information about you that is wrong or misleading.

Erasure: You can ask for your personal data to be deleted. If Purl refuses to delete your personal data, you will be told why.

Restriction: You can ask for the processing of your personal data to be restricted.

Objection: If we process information about you on the basis of a balancing of interests, you have the right to object to the processing.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated, and the new version is posted at the same address. If the changes are significant, we will tell you directly in the app. As we hold no contact details for you, we tell you about significant changes through an update to the app. The update tells you the next time you open the app with an internet connection.

Contact

Purl is the data controller for the app and potential personal data that you send to us. We are established in Norway.

Questions about privacy? You can contact us at privacy@purl.no.

If you live in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in the country where you live (in Norway, that is Datatilsynet).
