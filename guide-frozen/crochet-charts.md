# Crochet charts

Draw crochet charts with the symbols a pattern uses: chains, single
and double crochet, shells, puffs, popcorns and more. Draw by hand
with a finger or an Apple Pencil, or build whole rows and rounds from
written steps. A finished chart exports with a legend under it.

Crochet charts is a Beta. Using a crochet chart inside a pattern or a
project comes later.

## Opening it

Tap **More**, then **Crochet charts** in the **Tools** section.

The screen lists every crochet chart you have made, each with a small
picture and its number of stitches. Tap a chart to open it.

## Starting a chart

1. Tap **New crochet chart**.
2. Pick the drawing guides: **Circle**, **Square**, **Oval**,
   **Spiral**, or **None** for a blank page.
3. Change the numbers if you want to, such as **Rounds** and
   **Divisions**.
4. Tap **Start drawing**.

Type a name in the box at the top of the editor. Purl saves the chart
on its own while you work.

## Drawing stitches

The stitch buttons run along the bottom. Tap one to pick the stitch
you draw with. A new chart has the common stitches, from chain up to
double treble, plus the magic ring, decreases, a cluster, a puff, a
popcorn, a bobble and post stitches.

Then pick a tool in the tool row:

- **Stitch**: tap to place one stitch.
- **Line**: drag from start to end for a straight row of the stitch.
- **Curve**: draw freely. Close the loop to make a round.
- **Fan**: press where the fan starts, then sweep round. You get a
  shell, or a whole round from one point.
- **Eraser**: drag over stitches and labels to rub them out.

Line, Curve and Fan fill your stroke with evenly spaced stitches. On a
curve the stitches stand outward; on a straight line they stand up.

Right after you draw a row, a **Distribute evenly** pill shows over the
chart. Tap minus or plus to take one stitch out or put one in, and the
row spaces itself out again. Its Flip button turns the row upside down.

## Snap

The **Snap** pill sits at the top left of the chart. It starts on.

With Snap on, a new stitch stands on the top of the stitch below, the
way you would work it. While you draw, dots show the tops your stitches
will stand on. A row snaps whole or not at all. Chain spaces count as
places to work into.

A snapped stitch stays attached. Move, turn or resize the row below,
and the stitches on it follow.

Tap the pill to turn Snap off. With **Snap off**, each stitch goes
exactly where you draw it.

## Moving around the chart

- **Pinch with two fingers to zoom.**
- **Pick Move** (the hand) and drag with one finger to move the chart.
- **Touch a second finger down to cancel a stroke** you are part way
  through.
- **Use the buttons at the top right** to zoom in, zoom out, or show
  the whole chart.

If you draw with an Apple Pencil, Purl notices. The Pencil draws, a
finger moves the chart, and a resting hand is ignored. A pill says
**Pencil draws, your finger moves the chart**. Tap it to let your
finger draw again.

## Rows and rounds

**Rows and rounds** is the first button in the stitch row. It builds a
regular chart the way a pattern is written, then puts the stitches on
your chart for you to change by hand.

1. Pick the shape: **Rows**, **Rounds**, **Square** or **Oval**.
2. For Rows and Oval, set the **Foundation chain**. An oval is worked
   round both sides of it. Rounds and Square start from a magic ring.
3. Build the first row or round by tapping stitches, **Chains** and
   **Skip**.
4. Tap **New row** or **New round** for the next one.
5. Tap **Put on the chart**.

To build faster:

- **Stitches per tap** puts several stitches into one place with one
  tap. Pick 3, tap a double crochet, and you get "3 dc".
- **Into the same place** sends the next stitches where the last one
  went.
- **Repeat**: tap **[ Start here**, add the steps, then close the
  repeat with a count or with "in each". With no mark, the repeat takes
  the row so far.
- Tap a step under **Steps** to remove it.

Each row shows its written text as you build it, for example
"[3 dc, ch 2] 4 times". If a row works more or fewer stitches than the
row below has, it says so: "works 14 of 12". If a step has nothing to
work into, a line under the row says why and what to try.

A **Square** stays round until a round has four corners. Make them with
chains between four equal groups, as a granny square does. Or work 3
or more stitches into one stitch at four even places.

## Drawing guides

Guides are faint dotted rings and spokes under the stitches, like the
lines printed on paper for round charts. They help keep rounds even,
and they never show in an export.

Tap **Guides** at the top of the editor (the round target button) to
change them:

- **Circle**, **Square**, **Oval**, **Spiral** or **None**.
- **Rounds** and **Divisions**: how many rings and spokes. A spiral
  counts **Turns**.
- **Rounds of**: the stitch the rounds are made of, which sets the gap
  between rings. **Each round** lets one round use another stitch.
- **Snap to guides**: with Snap on, a stroke that does not stand on
  stitches is laid along the ring or spoke it is drawn on.
- **Centre on the stitches** moves the guides to the middle of what you
  have drawn.

## Selecting and changing stitches

There are two ways to select:

- Pick **Select** in the tool row, then tap stitches. Tap a stitch
  again to take it out. With nothing selected, a **Select all** pill
  shows over the chart.
- Hold a finger still on a stitch for half a second, then lift it. This
  works with Stitch, Line, Curve, Fan and Move. Tap the tick to finish,
  and you are back in the tool you had.

The Pencil never selects this way, so it can rest on a stitch before
you draw.

A selection gets a box around it. Drag inside the box to move it. The
round handle turns it, and the square handle resizes it. Resizing
spreads the stitches out or draws them in; the symbols keep their size.

The selection row has these buttons:

- **Whole row**: when on, a tap selects the whole row or round.
- **Change to**: makes every selected stitch the stitch you have picked.
- **Flip**: turns the selection upside down.
- **Delete** and **Select all**.
- **Round**, beside More: marks the selection as a round. See Rounds
  below.
- **More**: **Mirror left to right**, **Mirror top to bottom** and
  **Repeat around**.

**Repeat around** copies the selection round a centre point, like
slices of a cake. Set how many with minus and plus, tap the chart to
move the centre, then tap **Apply**.

## Rounds

A chart knows which stitches make each round or row, so you can follow
it round by round when you crochet. A chart made with **Rows and
rounds** has its rounds already, with their written text.

To mark rounds on a chart you drew:

1. Select the stitches of one round. **Whole row** on selects a whole
   stroke with one tap.
2. Tap **Round** in the selection row, then **New round** (or **New
   row**). For the magic ring or the first chain, pick **Start**.
3. Do the same for the next round. To add stitches to a round you
   already have, pick it under **Add to**. Stitches already in another
   round move, and a message says from where.

Tap **Rounds** at the top of the editor (the list button; the number
on it is how many) to see them all in order, each with its number,
its text and its stitch count, for example "47 sts, ch 3":

- The arrows move a round up or down. Numbers follow the order.
- Tap a round to give it your own **Number** (such as "3a") or
  **Text**, which shows in place of the written text. **Select its
  stitches** shows them on the chart. **Remove** takes the round off
  the list; the stitches stay.
- On a chart with no rounds, **Rounds from strokes** makes one round
  of each stroke, in the order you drew them.

Every change is one step that **Undo** takes back.

## Labels

Tap **Label** in the tool row. A pill over the chart offers three kinds:

- **Text**: tap the chart to write a label. Tap a label to change it.
- **Number**: tap to place the next number. Hold your finger on the
  chart to start again at 1.
- **Start**: tap where a round begins to mark it.

A label takes the colour you have picked.

## Colours

The colour row runs along the very bottom.

- Tap a colour, and the stitches you draw next are in it.
- With stitches selected, tap a colour to paint them. To colour a whole
  row, turn on **Whole row**, tap a stitch in it, then tap the colour.
- Tap **+** at the right to add a colour of your own, then **Use this
  colour**. The picker offers the same saved colours as Colour charts.
- Hold one of your own colours to take it off the row. Stitches already
  in that colour keep it.

## Your own stitches

Tap **Make a stitch** in the stitch row, then choose:

- **Height**: the stitch it is built on, such as dc.
- **Kind**: **Plain**, **Cluster**, **Puff**, **Popcorn**, **Bobble**,
  **Front post**, **Back post** or **Together (decrease)**.
- **How many**: for a cluster, puff, popcorn, bobble or decrease.

Tap **Add to palette**. The stitch gets its own button, ready to draw
with, and every chart you start later has it too.

Hold any stitch button for a small menu:

- **Edit**: change the stitch. If the chart already has some, Purl asks
  whether to change those too.
- **Remove from this chart**: stitches already drawn stay, and so does
  their line in the legend.
- **Arrange buttons**: drag the buttons into a new order.

With Purl in English, the **Terms** button at the end of the stitch row
switches the stitch names between **US** and **UK**.

## Undo

**Undo** and **Redo** are the arrows at the top of the editor. Every
change is one step: a whole row, a whole Flip, a whole repeat.

Undo keeps up to 200 steps while Purl is open. When you leave a chart,
its last 50 steps are kept, even if the device closes Purl.

## Folders, sharing and deleting

On the list, hold a chart to select it, then tap any others you want. A
bar at the bottom offers **Folder**, **Share** and **Delete**.

- **Folder**: pick a folder, or type a new folder name and tap
  **Create**. Folders then show above the list, each with **Add**,
  **Rename** and **Delete folder**. Deleting a folder keeps its charts.
- **Share**: sends one chart as a Purl file. Another Purl user opens it
  and gets a chart to edit, not just a picture. On an iPad or iPhone
  the file shows as a "Purl pattern".
- **Delete**: asks first, then moves the chart to **Recently deleted**
  in **More**, **Backups & history**, where it stays for 30 days.
  **Restore** there brings it back with its undo history. To delete one
  chart, you can also swipe it to the left or tap the bin on its row.

To bring in a chart file, tap **Import** on the list and pick the file,
or open the file in Purl from mail or Files. It is always added as a
new chart.

## Exporting

Tap **Export** at the top right of the editor. **Export chart** shows a
preview of the page and three formats:

- **Picture (PNG)**: print quality, 300 DPI on an A4 page.
- **Vector (SVG)**: sharp at any size, for design programs.
- **PDF**: one A4 page, ready to print.

Every page has the chart's name as its title and the legend under it.
**Legend names** changes how a stitch is named in the legend, in every
export. With Purl in English, **Stitch names** picks US or UK.

Then tap **Share**. On Android you can also **Save to device**.

## Tracking

Purl follows a crochet chart round by round as you work. Tracking needs
a pattern or a project to keep your place in, so it opens from there:

- **From a pattern**: open a pattern that holds a crochet chart and tap
  the charts button at the top. On the pattern's page in Patterns you can
  also tap the chart's picture under **Crochet charts**.
- **From a project**: make the crochet chart the project's pattern
  (**Choose a pattern**, then the chart), and tap **Open & track**.

The rounds you have worked are drawn solid, the rounds still to come are
faded, and the round you are on is highlighted. Under the chart you see
its number ("Round 4 of 22"), its stitch count ("47 stitches, ch 3") and
what to work. Tap **Next** when a round is done, **Previous** to go back.
Tap the round's number to see every round and jump to one, or hold a
stitch on the chart to go to its round. Pinch and drag to move around;
double tap to see the whole chart.

Your place is kept for each project, and the project's card shows it.
Just opening the tracker changes nothing. A chart with no rounds yet
opens too, and says how to add them. If the chart was edited since, Purl
stays on your round, or tells you where you are now if that round is
gone.

A chart a project uses as its pattern cannot be deleted. Purl names the
project, so you can take the chart out of it first.
