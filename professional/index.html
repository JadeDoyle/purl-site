Coming soon

Design the garment, not just the chart.

Purl Professional is a set of designer tools coming to Purl: SVG from every chart, written rows and a chart check for your patterns, a Garment Studio with your sweater in 3D, and knit charts with cables up to 8 stitches. Four of its makers already run in your browser.

Purl Professional is not on sale yet. What this page shows is built and in testing, and the four makers below let you try it now.

Try it in your browser

Each maker opens in your browser window, with nothing to install and no account. Pictures and PDFs you save from it are small and marked as a demo.

Built and in testing

Exports made for selling

SVG from every colour, knit and crochet chart, sharp at any size in your design program, for print and for your pattern's layout. Every Purl saves print-quality PNG and PDF with a small Made with Purl. mark at the foot; Professional lets you turn the mark off, so your exports go out without it.

SVGPNGPDF

  • Written rows from your chart

    Write out turns a colour or knit chart into rows, ready for your pattern. A knit chart reads in US, UK or Norwegian terms, with the repeats found for you: "*k2, p2; rep from * to last 2 sts, k2".

  • Chart check before your testers

    Check chart marks in red each knit chart row that does not work the stitches the row below leaves, and lists the rest: a repeat that does not join up, too many colours in a row, floats longer than your limit, a gap in the key.

  • Every size from one chart

    In the pattern creator, type a number once for each size and it prints 96 (104) 112 (120). Mark the stitches of a chart each size works, and its written rows cover every size at once, checked size by size. A chart too big for its page prints over several.

  • Versions of a pattern

    Every Save in the pattern creator keeps a version. History compares any two, change by change: the words, the table cells, the written rows and a chart's squares.

Garment Studio, in 3D

Start a sweater from a size and a gauge, raglan or drop shoulder, or a pair of mittens from a hand size. Paint each part stitch by stitch and watch the garment change in 3D beside the chart on an iPad, or in a corner on a phone.

  • Brush, eraser, fill and select, in one set of yarns for the whole garment.
  • Linked sleeves: paint one, the other follows as its mirror.
  • Ribbing that looks like ribbing, and felted mittens shown before and after washing.
  • Pen mode on the iPad: only the pencil paints, a resting palm paints nothing.
  • A PDF with every part, and one sleeve for a linked pair.

Mittens in 3D

Start a pair of mittens from a hand size and paint the hand and the thumb as two charts. The 3D shows the mitten in the shape it has when worn, with every stitch you paint, and its card in the Studio shows the pair.

The Studio on a phone

On a phone a small 3D of the garment sits in a corner of the chart and changes as you paint. Drag it out of the way, or show it large.

  • Your sweater on a body

    Drape hangs the sweater on a body of the size it is knitted for, from a child of 2 to a man's 5X. Try it on another size, or on a body made from your own measurements, and see the fit: loose on a smaller body, stretched on a larger one.

  • Amigurumi maker

    Design a toy round by round: shape each part, or read its rounds from a pattern, and see it take shape in 3D, leaning the other way if you crochet left-handed. Put the parts together and save the toy as a crochet pattern, with the stitch count for every round and the steps to sew it up.

Every chart in one place

One folder grid for colour, knit and crochet charts and your amigurumi toys. Tap a chart and its facts open at the side: size, repeat, colours, folder, and the Professional tools it uses.

  • One folder list for every chart kind.
  • Purl's chart makers sit beside the Professional tools, so you design from one place.

Crochet charts with a pencil

Rows, rounds, squares and ovals, or freeform at any angle. The Apple Pencil draws and a finger moves the chart. Purl writes each row in US, UK or Norwegian terms.

  • Draw your own stitch once and find it in every chart.

Knit charts with real cables

A larger knit library in Professional: over 40 more symbols, cables up to 8 stitches with twisted and purl crosses, and wide decreases drawn over the stitches they work.

  • Repeat boxes and row brackets.
  • Your own stitches shared across every knit chart.

Your knit chart, knitted in 3D yarn

Open the 3D view beside a knit chart and see it as fabric on the needle: cables cross, purls sit back and lace opens. It knits again as you draw, and opens full screen with a tap.

  • Every built-in knit stitch passed a 3D check before the view opened.
  • In the stitch library, each built-in knit stitch turns in 3D yarn under your finger.

Blueprints, placed with a tap

Save any part of a chart as a blueprint, a star, a cable or the rounds you always start with, and place it in another chart with a tap. Purl starts you off with motifs for colour, knit and crochet.

  • One library for all three chart kinds, sorted by recent, name or most used.
  • Placed in: every chart a blueprint sits in, and how many times.
  • Edit a copy of a starter and keep the original as it is.
  • Share a set of colour, knit or crochet blueprints as one .purlp file.

A stitch library you can browse

Every built-in stitch with its US and UK names and the charts it sits on, beside the stitches you made yourself.

Sketchpad, a drawing studio

A full drawing app inside Purl, for mood boards, design sheets and pattern pages. Draw on an endless board or in a book of A4 pages. Once the Apple Pencil touches the page, only the pencil draws, so a resting palm draws nothing.

  • Fine liner, ballpoint, pencil, brush, marker and highlighter, with pressure and tilt from the Apple Pencil, and pens you save.
  • Up to 20 layers, each with its own opacity, Multiply, hide and lock.
  • Lines, arrows, rectangles, ellipses and polygons that snap to a grid, a ruler and a set square, and a bucket fill that closes small gaps.
  • A lasso that moves, scales, turns, flips and copies, photos cropped to any shape, and text.
  • Grids in squares, dots or your knitting gauge, and 100% at true size on most iPads and iPhones.
  • Export a book as one PDF with sharp vector lines, ready to sell as a pattern, or as PNG pictures.

Your work stays yours

  • Offline

    No account and no server. Your charts live on your device.

  • Your files

    Back up everything to one .purl file and share charts and patterns as .purlp files. The formats are documented, so you can read your own data with other tools.

  • Shared charts always open

    Charts made with Professional tools still open and track without Professional, so a shared chart always works.
