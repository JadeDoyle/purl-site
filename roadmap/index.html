Roadmap

What Purl does today, grouped by area, and what is coming. This mirrors the Roadmap screen inside the app. One promise shapes it all: your data stays on your device, so nothing here will ever need an account.

In the app

Projects

Project tracking
from planned to finished, with yarn, sizes, dates, notes and photos
Several patterns in one project
a hat and its mittens together, each keeping its own notes and marks
A chart as the whole project
when a colour chart is the make, track it straight from the project, cell by cell
Video patterns
a YouTube pattern plays inside Purl beside your counters and notes, and remembers where you stopped
Timeline and gallery
your makes over time, and every photo in one place
Gallery by projects and patterns
your knits apart from pattern photos, filtered by in progress or finished
Stats
facts about your pace, yarn use and habits, next to the timeline
Pick up where you left off
the pattern you last worked from sits on top
Record the gauge you actually got
Counters on the project page
count without opening the pattern, even when it is only a link
Rename counters where you count
hold a counter's name under a video or a chart to rename or delete it
Folders
gather similar projects in a folder of their own, and the same for patterns
Hold to select
hold a project or a pattern, tap more, then file, delete, start or pause them all at once
Duplicate
hold a project, a pattern, a yarn or a chart and tap Duplicate for a copy to change a little, the original kept as it is

Yarn stash

Your yarn, organised
colours, dye lots, fibre and gauge, with totals and value
Photos of each yarn
the ball and the back of its band, with the front photo on the stash card
Scan a ball-band
a ball band you have scanned before fills in the details for you, colour and all
Scan to find
scan a ball band to open the yarn you have, then add a skein in one tap
New colours recognised
a ball band whose code differs from a yarn you know only in its last few digits is offered as that yarn in a new colour
Colours it remembers
a yarn colour you have used keeps its shade, so every later skein of it matches
Nordic yarn catalogue
autofills the main Nordic brands, over a thousand shades
Hold to select in the stash
hold a yarn or a tool, tap more, then delete them all at once
Shopping list
flag yarn or tools to buy, open the cart to see and clear them, share the list
Needles and hooks
an equipment stash by size and material
Weigh-in
weigh a ball to update how much is left
Search by the ball band
type "110m" or "50g" in the stash search to find the yarns that run about that per skein

Patterns

One pattern library
imported PDFs and your own written patterns together
Fold a pattern list
tap Your patterns or PDF patterns to fold it away, remembered on the device
Write your own
in sections, with abbreviations, photos, and a picture on any row
Print your own patterns
the text and every chart on A4 pages, from the print button in the reader
Made with Purl.
a small mark at the foot of every printed pattern and exported chart
Charts that are easier to see
export any chart in greyscale, inverted or large print
Draw colour charts
paint your own colourwork grid, see it as knitted or crocheted fabric, then follow it stitch by stitch
Yarn that looks like yarn
the 3D chart preview shows settled stitches with plies, fibres and a soft fuzz, each a little different in tone
The key beside the chart
while you follow a chart, its colours and symbols stay in view below it or beside it
A slim rail in phone landscape
turn the phone sideways and the tracker buttons move to the side, leaving the chart the screen
Colour charts save by themselves
undo after closing, copy and paste between charts, and Recently deleted
A key for colour charts
each colour's swatch, number and yarn or typed name, under the chart in every export and in the tracker
Charts from a spreadsheet
import an Excel chart: its coloured cells, its knitting symbol font or typed stitch names, and its key
Colourways for colour charts
one chart in up to twelve sets of colours side by side; switch, shuffle and swap, and knit the one on the canvas
Lasso select for crochet charts
draw a loop of any shape round the stitches you want, or keep the box
Space between crochet rounds
choose how much room a stitch leaves above the one below it, chart by chart
Space between stitches in a row
choose how far apart neighbouring crochet stitches sit, chart by chart
Lines keep the space
a drawn crochet line, chains included, spaces its stitches by your setting however long you draw it
Turn in steps, resize text
a crochet selection turns in 15 degree steps with Snap on and freely with it off; text and numbers resize
Lines start where you touch
a drawn crochet line's first stitch stays on the pen-down point, and rows snap onto widely spaced chains
The makers as one family
colour, knit and crochet charts start on the same page and keep Undo, Redo and More, with Export, in the same place
Big crochet charts draw fast
place a stitch, zoom and pan at once on charts of thousands of stitches
Daily snapshots keep your charts
colour, crochet and knit charts, a changed one coming back as a dated copy
Chart folders and sharing
file your charts in folders, hold one to select it, and send it as a Purl file another Purl user opens and edits
One set of chart folders
colour, knit and crochet charts share their folders, so one folder holds every chart for a design
Crochet charts (Beta)
draw crochet symbols by hand with Snap, or build rows and rounds from written steps, copy, paste, merge and unmerge rows, set exact stitch counts, and export the chart with a key
Crochet charts in patterns
keep a crochet chart inside a pattern, mark its rounds with their stitch counts, and track it round by round from the pattern or a project
Box select in crochet charts
drag a box round stitches to select them all at once
A legend in your own order
drag the stitches in a chart's legend into the order you want; the exports and the tracker follow it
Coloured symbols in the legend
give a stitch's symbol its own colour in the legend, so a marker you draw in red is red in the key
Your choice of crochet symbols
draw single crochet, slip stitch, chain, puff and popcorn in either of their two common styles, per chart
Rows that follow your chains
draw a rough stroke along a chain line or a row and every stitch lands on it; Flip mirrors the row to the other side
Crochet terms in German, Swedish and Danish
the legend, the stitch buttons and the written rows use each country's stitch names, as in Norwegian
Knit charts (Beta)
knit, purl, decreases and crosses on a grid, with row notes and a legend, followed row by row from a pattern; copy and paste between charts, mirror and share them, and make your own stitches in any shape, drawn or labelled, with a colour per square
Links in steps
a web address in a step of your own pattern opens with a tap
Copy a pattern you wrote
the same pattern again, in another size or another yarn, with its own pictures, charts and row counting
Duplicate a section
the second sleeve from the first, one tap in the pattern editor
A paper pattern into the app
photograph the pages, straighten and clean them up, and they become one PDF pattern
Count a chart as squares
coloured squares with a grid, on the swatch, so you can count stitches anywhere
Squares with no stitch
mark the squares where the knitting works no stitch, so a yoke or any chart that narrows as it climbs can be drawn
Import and share
patterns travel as files, no account needed
Send a PDF to Purl
from Books, Files, Mail or any share sheet, straight into your library
Match yarn and patterns
find a pattern for a yarn you own, or yarn for a pattern

Reading a PDF pattern

Works fully offline
big files welcome, pinch to zoom
Draw and highlight
pens, marker, eraser and lasso, with neat-shape help
Notes, bookmarks and pinned snippets
keep a chart key floating while you read
Row counters
several named counters, with targets and reminders
Chart stitch tracker
follow a colourwork or lace chart cell by cell
Find and navigate
page thumbnails, go to page, search the text
Night reading
A tools button you notice
it starts in the middle the first time, with a note that it moves and holds more tools

Tools

Craft calculator
gauge, resizing, even shaping, skeins needed, with lengths from your stash
Quick counters
for anything you count, with targets
Terminology glossary
knit and crochet terms in Norwegian and English; tap a term for a plain-language explanation with examples
Search everything
one box across projects, patterns, yarn and more

Your data

Everything stays on your device
no account, no server, nothing shared
Backup and restore
everything to a single file
Choose what you back up
every kind of thing has its own ticks, on the way out and on the way in
Recently deleted
a 30-day bin and snapshots to undo mistakes

Look and platform

Norwegian and English
German, Swedish and Danish
unofficial first translations, picked by hand in Settings, with a note on how they were made
Light and dark mode
Purl in your favourite colour
an optional purchase that sets the app to any colour
Seasonal logo
a witch hat in October and a knitted hat at Christmas, or the plain logo if you prefer
Made for iPad
a two-pane layout on big screens
Quick with a big library
Projects, Patterns and Stash switch at once with hundreds of yarns and patterns, and the chart makers list your charts sooner
Keyboard shortcuts on iPad and Mac
undo and redo in the chart makers, Escape and Return in dialogs
Trackpad pinch on a Mac
pinch to zoom about the pointer wherever a chart, a PDF or a photo shows; two fingers move it
Google Play
Purl on Android phones and tablets
User guide
a full written guide in Norwegian and English, opened in your browser
A problem does not close Purl
a screen that runs into trouble shows a card to reopen Purl, and what you saved is safe
One-time tips
small hints the first time you meet a hidden feature; bring them back from Settings
Keep the screen on
while you knit, on the pattern, the project page, the counters and the trackers, or everywhere in Purl
Support Purl
an optional tip
Rate Purl
a tile that takes you to the store page to leave a review
Rate without leaving
after a finished project, Purl may now and then ask for a rating inside the app
