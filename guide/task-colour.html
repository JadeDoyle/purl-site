Make a colour chart

Draw your own colourwork, one square per stitch. You can print it, or add it to a pattern.

  1. Tap More, then tap Colour charts.

  2. Tap New chart.

  3. Set Stitches (width) and Rows (height), then tap Make the chart.

  4. Pick a colour at the bottom and tap squares to paint them.
