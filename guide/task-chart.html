Follow a chart

Follow a printed chart in a PDF pattern, stitch by stitch. Purl lights up the stitch you are on and keeps your place.

  1. Open a PDF pattern, tap the round tool button, then tap Row tracker.

  2. In Trackers, tap Chart.

  3. Drag the box's corners over the printed chart and tap Looks good. Then type the Stitches across and the Rows, and tap Save grid.

  4. Step stitch by stitch with the side arrows.
