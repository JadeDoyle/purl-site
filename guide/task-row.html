Mark your row

Lay a coloured band over the row you are knitting in a PDF pattern. You move it on as you go, so you never lose your place.

  1. Open a PDF pattern, tap the round tool button, then tap Row tracker.

  2. In Trackers, tap Row counter. In the Counter sheet that opens, tap Set.

  3. Drag the band by its numbered tab onto the row you are on.

  4. Tap the down arrow when you finish a row.
